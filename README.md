# Rheinmetall_Dip_Analysis
Based on subjective observations, it is noticeable that the Rheinmetall share “frequently” makes a “dip” between 09:00 and 11:00. The purpose of the analysis is to check whether the “morning” dip at Rheinmetall shows a statistical repetition. And how the share performs in percentage terms after this dip up to the closing price or the daily high.
